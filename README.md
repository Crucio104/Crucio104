### About Me

Master's student in **Artificial Intelligence and Robotics (AIRO)** at **Sapienza University of Rome**, following completion of a Bachelor's degree in **Applied Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence (ACSAI)**, taught entirely in English.

Technical focus centers on multi-agent systems, computer vision, local LLM architectures, and robotics.

* **Academics:** M.Sc. in AIRO, Sapienza University of Rome • B.Sc. in ACSAI Graduate, Sapienza University of Rome. Maximum grades (30 and 30 cum laude) in university programming examinations for Python and Java.
* **Focus Areas:** Multi-Agent Systems, Local LLMs, Computer Vision (YOLO), Reinforcement Learning, Systems Architecture.
* **Collaborative Experience:** Technical activities and digital labs with **IBM**, **Ericsson**, and **Fondazione Mondo Digitale**.
* **Problem Solving:** Regional and national stages in Mathematics and Computer Science Olympics; maximum score in the TeraBebras competition.
* **Languages:** Italian (Native), English (B2 Certification).
