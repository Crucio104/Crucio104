### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a 3rd-year undergraduate student in **Applied Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence (ACSAI)** at **Sapienza University of Rome**, an international program taught entirely in English.

My core focus lies at the intersection of **Autonomous Multi-Agent Systems**, **Computer Vision**, **Local/Privacy-First LLMs**, and **Scalable Software Architecture**. I enjoy architecting systems that blend theoretical computer science with practical, modern engineering.

* 🎓 **Academics:** ACSAI @ Sapienza University of Rome. Maximum grades (**30 & 30 cum laude**) in foundational university programming examinations (Python & Java).
* 🤖 **Core Focus:** Multi-Agent Orchestration, Local LLMs, Computer Vision (YOLO), Reinforcement/Machine Learning, Systems Architecture.
* 💼 **Collaborative Experience:** Participated in technical labs and digital challenges with **IBM**, **Ericsson**, and **Fondazione Mondo Digitale**.
* 🏆 **Competitive Problem Solving:** Regional & National stages in **Mathematics and Computer Science Olympics**; achieved the maximum score in the **TeraBebras** competition.
* 🌍 **Languages:** Italian (Native) • English (B2 Certified & full-English academic curriculum).
